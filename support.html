How to play

Drag anywhere to steer the hollow; it swallows anything smaller than itself and grows as it eats. Slide over an edge and the cube rolls so the next face turns toward you; the six-face map shows what each face still holds. Gather the goal before the sun sets: finish with 40% of the daylight left for three facets, 20% for two. Settings → How to play repeats the lessons from the first islands.

Purchases

Quartz Shelf (15 islands) is free. The Full Atlas, bought once, opens the other four ranges (60 islands). The Prospector's Satchel adds two Glint, two Linger and two Gulp boosters. Bought the Full Atlas before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
