# Geode Roll, a puzzle for iPhone. Roll the island. Swallow the geode.

**Coming soon to the App Store.**

Steer a hollow across a floating stone cube. Swallow crystals to grow, slide over an edge to roll the next face into view, and gather what the island holds before Nightfall.

## A puzzle with six sides

- **The far side is out of sight.** A six-face map counts what each face still holds, so you plan the roll before you make it.
- **Start small, eat big.** Every crystal and stone you swallow widens the hollow, through eight sizes, until the geode heart fits.
- **Race the light.** Each island has 40–160 seconds of daylight. Finish with 40% left for three facets.

## Drag, roll, gather

1. **Drag anywhere to steer.** The hollow follows your thumb and swallows anything smaller than itself.
2. **Slide over an edge.** The whole cube rolls 90° and the next face turns toward you. Ridges and ponds block the way.
3. **Gather the goal before Nightfall.** Sooner earns more facets.

The 15 islands of Quartz Shelf are free. The Full Atlas ($3.99) opens the other 60 with one purchase. Glint, Linger and Gulp help when you are stuck; you find some every fifth island, and the Prospector's Satchel ($0.99) adds two of each.
