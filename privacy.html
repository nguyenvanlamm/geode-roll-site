GEODE ROLL

Privacy Policy

Geode Roll · last updated 18 November 2026

Geode Roll is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the facets earned on each island and how many Glint, Linger and Gulp boosters you hold.
  • Your settings: sound on or off, and whether the booster tip has been shown.
  • Whether you have bought the Full Atlas, and the store's ids of purchases already delivered, so none is counted twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The first range, Quartz Shelf (15 islands), is free. Purchases (the Full Atlas and the Prospector's Satchel) are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
